# 📊 E-Commerce Sales & Logistics Analytics

## 🎯 Business Problem
An e-commerce business needs to understand its true commercial performance. Top-line revenue metrics often hide operational inefficiencies, particularly in shipping and logistics. This project aims to identify profitable regions, track revenue trends, and pinpoint areas of margin leakage through return rate analysis.

## 🚀 Project Overview
This independent data analysis project processes 1,000 transactional records to extract actionable business insights. I utilized SQL for data extraction and aggregation, and Excel for data visualization and executive reporting.

## 🛠 Tech Stack
* **Database Engine:** MySQL
* **Visualization & Reporting:** Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Dashboards)
* **Concepts Applied:** Aggregation, Data Cleaning, Logistics Analysis

## 📂 Project Files
* `realistic_e_commerce_sales_data.csv`: The raw dataset containing transaction and logistics records.
* `sales_analysis.sql`: The MySQL scripts written to aggregate revenue by category, region, and shipping status.
* `Ecom_sales_data_analysis.xlsx`: The final interactive dashboard presenting the visual findings.

## 📊 Key Business Insights
1. **Category Performance:** Electronics drove the highest gross volume, indicating strong market demand for high-ticket items.
2. **Regional Strategy:** The West region outperformed others in total revenue, suggesting a highly effective regional distribution network.
3. **Logistics & Margin Leakage:** By analyzing the `Shipping Status` metric, I identified a significant volume of 'Returned' orders. This metric is critical because high return rates on premium electronics directly erode profit margins through reverse logistics costs. 

## 🧠 Strategic Conclusion
While top-line sales are strong, the business must optimize its fulfillment operations. Investigating the root cause of the high return rates—whether due to product defects, shipping damage, or inaccurate descriptions—will be the highest leverage action to improve net profitability.
